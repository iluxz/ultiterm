# code of conduct

## the short version

be cool. build cool stuff. don’t be an asshole.

## our standards

ultiterm should be a place where people can contribute, learn, and argue about terminal aesthetics without it turning into a dumpster fire.

examples of good behavior:

- being respectful and constructive
- giving useful feedback, not ego dumps
- assuming good intent, but owning mistakes
- focusing on what’s best for the project and users
- helping newcomers without being condescending

examples of not-okay behavior:

- harassment, hate speech, slurs, or personal attacks
- sexualized language or unwanted advances
- trolling, bait, flame wars, or bad-faith arguing
- doxxing or sharing someone’s private info
- spamming, derailing, or intentionally wasting maintainer time
- any other behavior that would make a reasonable person go “yeah, don’t do that”

## scope

this applies in project spaces - issues, PRs, discussions, commits, reviews, and anywhere you’re representing ultiterm.

## enforcement

project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

if someone’s being out of pocket, maintainers can edit/remove/reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions, and can temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## reporting

if you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it by opening a private security/advisory channel if available, or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub.

for repo maintainer: **@iluxz**

include as much context as you can (links, screenshots, timestamps). reports will be reviewed and handled as discreetly as possible.
